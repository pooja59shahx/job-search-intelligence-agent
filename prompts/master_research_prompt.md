# Master Internship Research Prompt

## Purpose

This prompt powers the deep-research stage of the Job Search Intelligence Agent.

The objective is to discover, verify, evaluate, and prioritize internship opportunities based on a candidate's professional background, skills, location preferences, and career goals.

## Primary Objective

Optimize for:

**Interview Probability × Role Quality × Full-Time Conversion Potential ÷ Candidate Time**

The system should prioritize a smaller number of highly relevant opportunities rather than maximizing application volume.
