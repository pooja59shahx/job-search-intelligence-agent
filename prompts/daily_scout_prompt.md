# Daily Internship Scout

## Purpose

Run a lightweight recurring search for newly posted internship opportunities without repeating the entire deep-research process.

## Daily Process

1. Search for newly posted internships.
2. Check existing priority companies.
3. Discover opportunities at companies outside the existing watchlist.
4. Prioritize San Francisco, Bay Area, and remote-California opportunities.
5. Verify that each job is currently active.
6. Compare results against previously discovered jobs.
7. Remove duplicates.
8. Evaluate strong new opportunities against the candidate profile.
9. Surface only genuinely relevant opportunities.

## Priority Functions

- Revenue Operations
- Marketing Operations
- Growth Operations
- Growth Marketing
- Lifecycle / CRM Marketing
- Marketing Analytics
- GTM Operations
- Sales Operations
- Business Operations
- Revenue Strategy

## Freshness

Prioritize opportunities posted within:

- Last 24 hours
- Last 3 days
- Last 7 days

## Qualification

Strong opportunities should include:

- Fit score
- Location
- Date posted
- Verification status
- Why the candidate fits
- Key gaps
- Direct application link
- Recommended contact
- LinkedIn profile
- Verified professional email when publicly available
- Outreach draft
- Resume-tailoring recommendations

Never fabricate contact information, experience, skills, or job availability.

## Output Rule

Only surface:

- NEW opportunities
- Material updates to previously identified opportunities
- Previously monitored companies that have opened relevant positions

If there are no strong new matches, report that clearly rather than generating filler recommendations.
